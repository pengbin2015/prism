Why can the errors diverge?

A flexible curve can follow noise in the training observations. New observations contain different noise.

Is the validation winner guaranteed?

No. It is the best observed candidate on this validation sample. Repeated tuning can also overfit that sample.

Where can data leakage enter?

Using validation or test targets during fitting leaks evaluation information. Fit preprocessing on training data too.

What does this browser actually fit?

A polynomial in a Chebyshev basis. It minimises training squared error plus lambda times squared nonconstant coefficients. A tiny penalty stabilises the “almost none” option.
